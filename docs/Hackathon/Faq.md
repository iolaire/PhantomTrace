
QUESTION	ANSWER
Do I need to pay for Kiro?	
No. You can request 4,000 free Kiro credits. Request them on the Resources tab by December 1 at 12:00 PM PT. We review requests and send credits within 48 business hours.

How do I redeem my Kiro credits?	
Create a Kiro account if you do not have one. Do this before you open the redemption URL.
Log in to your Kiro account. Then open the URL to redeem your credits.
Look for a green message that says “Kiroupon successfully redeemed!”
Do you see an error instead? The redemption often worked anyway. Log out, wait a few minutes, and log back in. Then check your usage dashboard (step 4) before you contact us.
Within 1-2 minutes, the credits appear on your usage dashboard at kiro.dev/account/usage 
Redeem your code by Friday, December 4, 2026. We cannot replace expired codes.
Redeemed credits stay on your account until you use them or until they expire on December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT.
Do I have to use Kiro?	
Yes. Your project must be a working software application that uses Kiro (vibe coding, agent hooks, steering docs, MCP, or specs). Your repo must also contain the /.kiro directory at the root.

Can I use a project I started before October 30?	
No. Only new projects created during the Submission Period are eligible. This includes projects from Kiroween 2025.

Can I use open source libraries or code?	
Yes, if you follow their licenses. Your project must build on and enhance them. Your submission must be your own original work. See “Intellectual Property” in the Official Rules.

How big can my team be?	
Up to 3 people. One person (the Representative) submits for the team.

Can I submit to more than one category?	
No. Each project goes into one category. You can also apply for any bonus prizes you qualify for.

Does my code repository need to be public?	
Yes. It must be a public GitHub repository. We do not accept GitLab or Bitbucket. It needs an open source license visible in the About section. It must include the /.kiro directory. Do not add /.kiro to your .gitignore.

Does my project need to be live?	
You must provide a URL to your working application. If judges need to log in, add test credentials and instructions to the submission form. Only judges can see this field.

How long should my video be?	
Under three minutes. Judges are not required to watch beyond three minutes. Upload it to YouTube and make it public.

Can my project win more than one prize?	
Yes. A project can win one Overall Prize or one Category Prize, not both. It can also win any number of Bonus Prizes.

Can I edit my submission after the deadline?	
No. Save drafts and submit before December 4, 2026 at 2:00 PM PT.
